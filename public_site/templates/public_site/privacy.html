This Privacy Policy describes how Neighbor Service App (“NSA,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile applications, website, and related services (together, the “Platform”). Using the Platform means you agree to the practices described here and in our Terms & Conditions.

Section 01

Overview

In plain terms

We're a marketplace, not the people doing the work. We collect what we need to run the app, keep it safe, and connect neighbors with screened help — and nothing more than that.

Neighbor Service App is a technology platform that connects individuals seeking services (“Seekers”) with independent service providers (“Providers”). We don't perform services, employ Providers, or supervise the work done through the Platform. Because we sit in the middle of these connections, we handle account, communication, and transaction information so the marketplace can function.

This policy applies to everyone who uses the Platform — Seekers, Providers, and visitors to our website. Where a section applies to only one type of user, we say so.

Section 02

Information we collect

In plain terms

Three buckets: what you give us (like your name and profile), what your device tells us automatically (like how you use the app), and what trusted partners share with us (like the result of a background screening).

Information you give us. When you create an account or use the Platform, you may provide:

  • Account details such as your name, email address, phone number, and password.
  • Profile information, including a photo, a description of services, service areas, and availability.
  • Communications you send through the Platform, such as messages between Seekers and Providers, and messages to our support team.
  • Ratings, reviews, and other content you choose to post.
  • Payment-related information you submit to complete a transaction (handled by our payment processor — see Payments).

Information we collect automatically. When you use the Platform, we may collect:

  • Device and connection details, such as device type, operating system, app version, and IP address.
  • Usage information, such as the listings you view, the searches you run, and the features you use.
  • Approximate or precise location, when you enable it (see Location data).
  • Cookies and similar technologies on our website (see Cookies & tracking).

Information from third parties. We may receive information about you from:

  • Third-party login providers such as Apple or Google, when you sign in with them.
  • Background-screening and identity-verification partners — for example, Checkr — when a check is requested. Every Provider completes third-party screening before activation.
  • Payment processors such as Stripe, which confirm transaction status without sharing full card numbers with us.

Section 03

How we use information

In plain terms

We use your information to run the marketplace, match you with the right neighbor through Smart Search, keep accounts secure, and improve the app. We don't sell your personal information.

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and verify eligibility (you must be at least 18).
  • Power Smart Search and connect Seekers with screened Providers — weighing availability, distance, ratings, and response time.
  • Enable messaging, scheduling, and the NSA Security Code that verifies arrival and job completion.
  • Process payments and subscriptions, and support promotional placement features for Providers.
  • Arrange identity verification or background screening through independent vendors.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and activity that violates our Terms.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • Send service messages and, where permitted, updates about features and promotions.
  • Analyze usage to maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Platform.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as described in the next section.

Section 04

How we share information

In plain terms

To connect a Seeker and a Provider, each side sees what they need to coordinate. Beyond that, we only share with the vendors who help run the app, or when the law requires it.

With other users. When a Seeker and Provider connect, certain details are shared so you can coordinate — such as your name, profile, ratings, and messages. Don't share sensitive information you don't want another user to see.

With service providers. We share information with vendors that perform services for us, such as:

  • Payment processors (for example, Stripe) to handle on-platform transactions.
  • Background-screening and identity-verification vendors (for example, Checkr), when a check is requested.
  • Cloud hosting, analytics, customer support, and communications providers.

For legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information when we believe it is reasonably necessary to comply with a law, regulation, or legal request; to enforce our Terms; or to protect the rights, safety, and property of users, the public, or NSA.

In a business transfer. If NSA is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Off-platform activity. If you and another user communicate or pay outside the Platform, that activity is not controlled by us and is not covered by this policy.

Section 05

Third-party logins

In plain terms

Signing in with Apple or Google is your choice. They share a limited set of details with us so you can log in without a separate password.

You can register or sign in using third-party providers such as Apple or Google. When you do, that provider shares basic account information with us — such as your name and email address — based on the permissions you grant. Your use of those providers is governed by their own privacy policies. You can manage or revoke these connections in your account settings with that provider.

Section 06

Payments

In plain terms

Payments made through the app are handled by a payment processor like Stripe — we don't store your full card number. Payments made outside the app are between you and the other person.

Payments for services may be made through the Platform using supported payment processors, or outside the Platform using methods the parties choose. For on-platform payments, a third-party processor such as Stripe collects and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive confirmation of transaction status but do not store complete payment card numbers.

NSA does not control or monitor payments made outside the Platform and is not responsible for disputes, fraud, or losses arising from off-platform transactions.

Section 07

Location data

In plain terms

Location helps match you with nearby help and show service areas. You control it through your device settings, and you can turn it off.

Because many services happen at a specific address, location information helps us show relevant listings, define service areas, and improve matching. Depending on your settings, we may collect approximate location (from your IP address) or precise location (from your device, with your permission). You can enable or disable location access at any time in your device or browser settings, though some features may not work as well without it.

Section 08

Cookies & tracking

In plain terms

Our website uses cookies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can manage them in your browser.

We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies to operate and secure the Platform, remember your preferences, and measure how our services are used. Some cookies are necessary for the site to function; others help us understand usage and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and disabling some may affect how the website works.

Section 09

Your privacy choices

In plain terms

You can view and update your profile, manage notifications, and ask us to access or delete your information. Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights.

You have choices about your information:

  • Access and update. Review and edit much of your information directly in your account settings.
  • Delete your account. You may close your account at any time; some information may be retained as described in Data retention.
  • Communications. Opt out of promotional messages using the unsubscribe link or your settings. We may still send essential service messages.
  • Device permissions. Manage location, notifications, and similar permissions in your device settings.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights — such as the right to access, correct, delete, or port your information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details in Contact us. We will respond consistent with applicable law and may need to verify your identity first.

Section 10

Data retention

In plain terms

We keep information for as long as your account is active and a while after, where we need it for legal, safety, or record-keeping reasons.

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Platform. After you close your account, we may retain certain information to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we take steps to delete or de-identify it.

Section 11

Security

In plain terms

We work to protect your information, but no system is perfectly secure. Keep your password private and tell us right away if something looks wrong.

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, and misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential. If you believe your account has been accessed without authorization, notify us immediately.

Section 12

Children's privacy

In plain terms

The Platform is for adults. It isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

The Platform is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from a person under 18, we will take steps to delete it. If you believe a minor has provided us with information, please contact us.

Section 13

Changes to this policy

In plain terms

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top and, for bigger changes, let you know in the app.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, provide additional notice through the Platform. Your continued use of the Platform after an update means you accept the revised policy.
